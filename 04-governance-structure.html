Day 4 · Governance structure

Week 1 · Foundations · ~45–60 min

Learning goals

Design ownership across leadership, Legal, Privacy, Security, Product, Engineering, Risk and Operations.

Three layers

Enterprise governance sets risk appetite, policy and accountability. Portfolio governance inventories and triages use cases, assigns risk tiers and routes reviews. System governance defines controls, evidence, owners, monitoring and incidents for a specific use.

Typical responsibilities

Executives: oversight, risk appetite, resources. Business/Product: accountable use-case owner. Engineering/Data: implementation, testing, documentation. Legal/Privacy: obligations and legal/privacy risk. Security: threats, access and abuse. Risk/Compliance: framework, challenge, assurance. Operations: workflows, human review, escalation, monitoring and incidents.

Committees don't replace owners

A committee coordinates and challenges, but every system still needs an accountable business owner and named control owners. Decision rights and escalation thresholds should be explicit.

Your consulting angle: Organizations often have Legal guidance and technical teams but lack the operating layer connecting them.

Apply it

Customer-service agents use an LLM that retrieves account information and drafts answers. Assign an accountable owner and five participating functions. What decisions belong to each?
